    Advanced Structures: Segment Tree, Fenwick Tree & Union-Find

    Segment and Fenwick trees precompute partial answers over ranges so that "sum/min of a[l..r]" and "change a[i]" both take O(log n), and union-find keeps track of which items are in the same group while groups keep merging.
