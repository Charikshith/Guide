    How to read it

    1. Vol 0 · MathStart here. Logic, counting, Big-O, probability and linear algebra. Needed for algorithms (Vol 1 Part 4) and for all of Vol 5.
    2. Vol 1 · CS FoundationsThe base for everything: code, tools, algorithms, machines, databases and cloud. Vol 1 Parts 1–3 feed every later volume.
    3. Vol 2 · Software Eng.How code ships and survives production: reviews, CI/CD, security, incidents.
    4. Vol 3 · Low-Level DesignDesign clean components with OOP, SOLID, patterns and architecture styles. Requires Vol 2.
    5. Vol 4 · High-Level DesignDesign whole systems that scale. Needs Vol 1 (Parts 7–9) and Vol 3. Ends with 30+ designs.
    6. Vol 5 · AI SystemsModels, serving, RAG, agents and production. Needs Vol 0 (linear algebra, probability) and Vol 1 (encoding, Ch 9).

    Key takeaways

    Depth grows step by step

    Math and code first, then engineering, then design, then whole systems and AI.

    Not strictly linear

    Vol 1 Parts 1–3 feed everything, and Vol 0 gates Vol 5. Each chapter lists its own prerequisites.

    Each volume has an exit goal

    For example Vol 4: design 30+ real-world systems. Vol 5: the 7 progressive builds.

    Simplified: chapters are grouped by theme, and the ranges are chapter numbers inside each volume. Single chapter titles are left out; see the contents on the home page.
